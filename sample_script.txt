**EXT. MOUNTAIN FOREST TRAIL – SUNSET**

A narrow trail winds through towering pine trees. Golden sunlight filters through the branches as mist drifts between the trunks. Fallen leaves cover the ground.

ELENA (late 20s, determined), KAI (30s, rugged), and MIRA (20s, curious) hike cautiously through the forest.

Elena studies an old leather map while Kai scans the surroundings with a pair of binoculars. Mira carries a weathered backpack with a lantern hanging from its side.

A flock of birds suddenly bursts from the trees.

The three freeze.

KAI
Something's moving.

A low rumble echoes in the distance.

Elena folds the map and points toward an abandoned stone watchtower visible on a nearby ridge.

The group quickly heads uphill.

---

**INT. ABANDONED WATCHTOWER – DUSK**

Dust floats through beams of fading sunlight entering broken windows.

Ancient books, rusted weapons, shattered furniture, and faded banners lie scattered across the circular room.

Kai lights the lantern.

The warm glow reveals strange symbols carved into the stone floor.

Mira kneels beside a wooden chest covered in dust.

She carefully opens it.

Inside are a brass compass, an engraved silver key, and a cracked journal.

ELENA
Take the journal.

Kai notices fresh muddy footprints leading toward a narrow spiral staircase.

The lantern flickers.

The room falls silent.

The three exchange nervous glances before climbing the stairs.

---

**EXT. WATCHTOWER ROOFTOP – NIGHT**

Dark storm clouds gather overhead.

Rain begins to fall.

The rooftop overlooks an enormous valley illuminated by distant lightning.

Ancient statues stand around a circular stone platform.

Wind whips Elena's coat as she places the silver key into a stone pedestal.

The platform slowly begins to glow with faint blue light.

Kai raises his flashlight toward the surrounding cliffs.

Several distant silhouettes stand motionless among the rocks.

MIRA
We're not alone.

The glowing platform emits a low hum.

Lightning flashes across the sky as the three stand together, staring into the darkness.
