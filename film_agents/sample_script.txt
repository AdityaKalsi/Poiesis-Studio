INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT

SARAH (30s, exhausted) stares at a letter on the table. She pours whiskey
into a chipped mug, hands trembling. Headlights sweep across the window --
someone's outside.

Sarah quickly folds the letter and shoves it into her coat pocket. She
opens a drawer, pulls out an old revolver, and sets it on the counter
within reach.

INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

The front door creaks open. MARCUS (40s, soaked from rain) steps into the
hallway, calling out.

MARCUS
Sarah? It's me.

Sarah's hand hovers near the revolver. She recognizes his voice and
relaxes, but only slightly. She slides the gun back into the drawer
before he enters the kitchen.

MARCUS (CONT'D)
(seeing her face)
What happened?

SARAH
Nothing happened. Where were you?

Marcus doesn't answer. He notices the whiskey, the trembling hands, the
outline of the letter in her pocket. A long beat of silence between them.
